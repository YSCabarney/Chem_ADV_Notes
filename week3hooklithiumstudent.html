4Why It's in the News

Fast facts — the "white gold" rush

  • South America's "Lithium Triangle" (Chile, Argentina, Bolivia) sits on more than half of the world's known lithium resources — mostly as brine under salt flats.
  • Pond evaporation is cheap but slow: it can take a year or more of desert sun to concentrate one batch, and it uses huge amounts of water in already-dry regions.
  • The U.S. is chasing its own supply: companies are building plants to pull lithium from super-hot geothermal brine at California's Salton Sea ("Lithium Valley").
  • New "direct lithium extraction" (DLE) tech tries to grab the lithium in hours instead of months — a physical separation race worth billions.
  • Prices have swung wildly as electric-vehicle demand rises and new mines come online — classification of matter, but make it geopolitics.
