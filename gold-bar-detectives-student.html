The situation

Gold is at record prices — and so is gold fraud

Counterfeiters have been drilling out real gold bars, packing the core with tungsten, and resealing them with a thin layer of real gold. A scale alone can't catch it, because tungsten's density (19.25 g/cm³) sits almost exactly on top of gold's (19.30 g/cm³).

You're the inspection team. Nobody is going to hand you a clean "volume" number — you'll have to build it yourself from whatever measurements each bar came with, and decide whether every number on the page actually belongs in your calculation.

Your tool

The formula

density = mass ÷ volume

Density is measured in grams per cubic centimeter (g/cm³). 1 cm³ = 1,000 mm³. 1 mL = 1 cm³.
